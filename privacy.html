Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy is also available in Spanish (Español), French (Français) and Portuguese (Português). In case of conflict, the Spanish version shall prevail for users located in Mexico.

MexiGuide ("we", "us", or "our") is operated by Rumbo Tech Labs, S.A.S. (Mexico). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the MexiGuide mobile application.

1. Information we collect

Account Information

When you create an account using Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo. We store a unique user ID to manage your account.

Location Data

With your permission, we use your device's location to show nearby safety zones, hospitals, and your position on the map. Safety zone alerts are worked out on your phone (and, in the Apple Watch app, on the watch): your position is not sent to our servers for them. Background location is used only for safety zone alerts, and only when you enable this feature.

When you use AI tips or Identify, an approximate location (rounded to about 100 meters) is sent to our server to tailor the result. The ride safety check sends your start and destination points to our server, and the destination you type to OpenStreetMap to find it on the map.

On iPhone, the map shows the current weather. To get it, your position rounded to about 11 km is sent to Apple WeatherKit, at most about every 30 minutes. It is never your exact location and it is not sent to our servers.

Camera & Photos

When you use the Identify feature, your photos are sent to our server for AI analysis. Images are processed in real-time and are not stored permanently on our servers.

Usage Data

We collect usage analytics (via Firebase Analytics), tied to a random app ID rather than your name, to understand how the app is used and to improve your experience. This includes screen views, feature usage, and crash reports. You can turn this off at any time.

2. How we use your information

  • Display your location on safety maps and trigger zone alerts
  • Process images through AI services (Anthropic Claude) for the Identify feature
  • Provide personalized travel recommendations
  • Track your travel milestones and goals
  • Manage your subscription and purchases (via RevenueCat / Apple / Google)
  • Send push notifications for safety advisories (when enabled)
  • Improve the app through usage analytics (unless you turn them off)

3. Third-party services

We share data with the following services, strictly for app functionality:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, crash reporting, analytics
  • Supabase: Database and backend services
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): Turns a typed destination or an approximate photo location into a place name
  • MapTiler: Map images (their servers see which area of the map you are viewing)
  • Apple WeatherKit: Current weather on the iPhone map (receives your position rounded to about 11 km)
  • Anthropic: AI image analysis and travel tips (receives the photo or an approximate location; images processed in real-time, not stored)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and purchase management
  • Apple / Google: In-app purchases and payment processing

Affiliate partners (Airalo, SafetyWing, Wise, NordVPN, GetYourGuide, Booking.com) are linked from within the app. Clicking an affiliate link takes you to their website; we do not share your personal data with them.

4. Data storage & security

Your data is stored on secure servers managed by Supabase (AWS infrastructure) and Firebase (Google Cloud). We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

5. Data retention

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account
  • AI-processed images: Not stored permanently: processed in real-time only
  • Analytics data: Retained for up to 14 months (Firebase default)
  • Location data: Used in real-time only and never saved in our database. Server logs may briefly keep a rounded position (about 1 km) to fix errors.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data (via Profile > Delete Account)
  • Opt out of usage analytics and crash reports (via Profile > Share usage and crash data)
  • Clear your trip data on this phone (via Profile > Clear my trip data)
  • Withdraw location permissions at any time via your device settings
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting us

These rights apply under the Mexican Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data (LFPDPPP), the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Apple/Google store policies.

How to exercise your rights: Send a request to privacy@mexiguide.org including your full name, the email associated with your account, a description of the right(s) you wish to exercise, and any supporting documentation. We will respond within 20 business days, as required by Mexican law.

7. Children's privacy

MexiGuide is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. This website

This website (mexiguide.org) has no accounts and does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. The safety map page loads map images from a map provider, whose servers see which area you are viewing. Links to partners (Airalo, Booking.com, SafetyWing, Wise and others) take you to their websites; we may earn a commission, and we do not share your personal data with them.
