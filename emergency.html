Important Safety Tips

  • ✅
    Save 911 before traveling: Add Mexico's emergency number to your phone contacts immediately upon arrival.
  • ✅
    Keep your embassy on speed dial: Critical for passport emergencies, legal issues, or when local authorities can't help.
  • ✅
    Use SECTUR for tourist support: The 078 line connects you with English-speaking assistance 24/7.
  • ✅
    Look for Policía Turística: Tourist police in major destinations speak English and patrol tourist zones.
  • ✅
    Screenshot this page: Save it to your phone for offline access in case you lose service.
  • ✅
    Share with travel companions: Make sure everyone in your group has these contacts saved.
