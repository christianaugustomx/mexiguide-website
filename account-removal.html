Request the deletion of your MexiGuide account and all associated data.

What happens when you delete your account

When your account removal request is processed, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your profile information (name, email, profile photo)
  • Saved destinations and travel preferences
  • Bookmarked city guides and favorites
  • App usage history and settings
  • Any other data associated with your account

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Account removal is typically processed within 7 business days.
